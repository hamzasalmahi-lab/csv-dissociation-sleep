# CSV Dissociation Study — CII-PDS Semi-Independence Across Sleep Stages

**Pre-registration:** https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/CRFJE

**Dataset:** CAP Sleep Database, PhysioNet
https://physionet.org/content/capslpdb/1.0.0/

**Framework:** HRIT v3
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19490741

## Hypotheses

H1 — CII stratification: permutation entropy stratifies Wake > REM ≈ N1 > N2 > N3

H2 — PDS stratification: HEP peak-to-peak amplitude is lower in REM than Wake
despite comparable EEG complexity

H3 — The dissociation: within-subject CII-PDS correlation is significantly lower
during REM than during NREM

H4 (exploratory) — RBD subjects show smaller CII-PDS dissociation in REM than
healthy controls due to absent atonia
